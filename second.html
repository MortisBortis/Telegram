Telegram is a cross-platform instant messaging system (messenger) with VoIP functions that allows you to exchange text, voice and video messages, stickers and photos, and files in many formats. You can also make video and audio calls and broadcasts in channels and groups, organise conferences, multi-user groups and channels. With the help of bots, the functionality of the application is virtually unlimited. Telegram client applications are available for Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and GNU/Linux. As of January 2021, the service has approximately 500 million monthly active users. In August 2017, Pavel Durov stated on his Telegram channel that the number of messenger users is increasing by more than 600,000 every day.
